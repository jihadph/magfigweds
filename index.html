Our Story

Jihad and Tintin’s story began at Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology, where they both pursued a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering.

Though they came from different places, their paths crossed in college. Tintin is from Lugait, Misamis Oriental, and spent much of her childhood between Naawan and Lugait. Jihad is from Ramain, Lanao del Sur, but spent most of his childhood in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

From classmates and fellow engineering students to building their own careers abroad as OFWs, the years have taken them far from where their story first began. Now, after growing together through different seasons of life and finding success in their respective careers, Jihad and Tintin have chosen to begin their next chapter together as husband and wife.

FAQs
What is the dress code?
The recommended dress code is modest smart casual. We’d love for our guests to feel comfortable while celebrating with us. For a little guidance, please refer to this Color Palette .
What serves as entry to the venue?
Upon arrival, please present your digital invitation to our wedding coordinator. This will serve as your entry to the venue.
Will food be served?
Yes. A meal will be served during the reception, along with refreshments and desserts.
Will the food be halal?
Yes, all food served at our wedding reception will be Halal.
What if I can no longer attend after confirming?
We completely understand that circumstances can change. Please let us know as early as possible so we can plan and adjust our seating and catering arrangements.
Will there be assigned seating?
Yes. A seating plan will be prepared for the reception. Please check the seating guide when you arrive at the venue.
Is there a gift registry?
Your presence at our wedding is already the greatest gift we could ask for. We are not having a traditional gift registry, but for those who may wish to give a gift, we can arrange a designated gift area during the event. For those who prefer to give cash, you may send via our GCash . Please know that this is completely optional, and simply celebrating this special day with us means so much.
